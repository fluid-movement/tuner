User (2026-11-24): build all recommendations of the UX research (core loop: tune → play → aim at a chord → land it → aim at the next), Claude decides the open points. Scope: (1) target chord: tapping a Try-next idea, a history chord, the chord of the day or Play it in the lookup pins a target on the listening card while it listens; (2) a quiet landed moment: green name, one soft ring, short vibration where supported, reduced motion respected, no sound; (3) tuning ends: all strings in tune shows All in tune, the sheet closes and the card says Ready; (4) first run explains before the microphone prompt (Start listening), denied access explains how to re-enable; (5) chord-change time between two different chords (Am → F · 1.4 s), best per pair kept; (6) my chords: chords you have played are remembered with the date, a quiet new tag the first time, the lookup marks played chords; (7) Learn reacts to what you played (explains the move when a target lands); (8) chord of the day, picked from the date, just beyond what you have played; (9) a weekly practice view (days played, minutes) and a short summary of the last session on return, no streak; (10) one-minute changes drill: two chords, 60 s, count clean changes, best per pair, suggest slow pairs; (11) play along a progression in a key: the current chord is the target and it moves on when landed. Everything local (localStorage), offline, calm look.
