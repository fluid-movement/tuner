## Requirement

User (2026-11-20), on the deployed version (before the confidence gate): chord mode shows chords when no note is played; the threshold is too low. Chord mode must stay quiet for everyday sounds when the ukulele isn't played: talking, whistling, mains hum and fans, a quiet room with small clicks and taps, as well as the noise bursts already covered. Real strums, including fairly quiet ones (a phone at arm's length), must still be recognised.
