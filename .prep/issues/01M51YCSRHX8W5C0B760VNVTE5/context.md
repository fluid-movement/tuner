- `crates/tuner-core/src/tracker.rs`: `NoteTracker` fed with per-frame `PitchEstimate`s (now including frame RMS) from a permissive detector (no absolute gate beyond digital silence, no clarity cut). States Idle / Tracking / Holding. Noise floor in dB: falls fast, rises slowly and only while not tracking. Onset: clarity ≥ ~0.85 and level ≥ floor + ~12 dB. Keep: clarity ≥ ~0.5, level ≥ floor + ~4 dB, within ~35 cents of the tracked pitch after octave folding. Retarget on a confident reading far from the tracked pitch for two frames or a fresh level jump. Release after ~250 ms of misses into Holding (~3 s), then Idle. Smoothing (median of 5 in cents + EMA ~120 ms, reset on retarget) moves here from `web/src/lib/tuner/smoother.ts`; the in-tune latch stays in TS.
- `crates/tuner-wasm`: the handle runs detector + tracker per frame; getters for state, smoothed and raw frequency, clarity, level and floor.
- Worklet posts tracker output; the page dims held readings, adds a level meter and a `?debug` overlay.
- Tests: tracker scenarios on synthetic plucks (quiet/noisy levels, mic low cut, noise-only, string change, hold timing) plus a `track_eval` example reporting display coverage; a decaying-pluck fake-mic project in Playwright.
