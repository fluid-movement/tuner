Theory: `web/src/lib/theory/key.ts` `suggestNext` returns `root`, `suffix` and `reason` per idea; reasons come from `nextReason(mode, from, to)` (specific moves like V→I, then what the target degree does in the key). `lookupTarget` in `web/src/lib/theory/lookup.ts` maps an analysed chord to a lookup entry.
UI: `LearnToggle.svelte` (corner of the chord and lookup cards); `ChordView.svelte` and `ChordLookup.svelte` take `learn` (bindable); ideas show `ChordDiagram` with `tiny`; tapping an idea or a history chord calls `onLookup`. `web/src/routes/+page.svelte` keeps `learn` (localStorage `tuner.learn`, default off) and the lookup chord (`tuner.lookup`, moved from ChordLookup) and switches to lookup mode.
