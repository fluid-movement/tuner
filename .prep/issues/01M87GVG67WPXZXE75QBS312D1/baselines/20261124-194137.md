User (2026-11-24). A Learn switch (one setting, remembered, default off = Simple) on the chord-mode and lookup cards. Simple shows a clean view: chord mode keeps name, diagram, tab, intonation warning, history and next-chord ideas; lookup keeps name, diagram, tab and shapes on the neck. Learn adds the explanations: chord description and other names, note roles under the diagram, notes and keys, the detected key and confidence, Roman numerals in the history, and for each next-chord idea why it works (e.g. 'V → I · the dominant resolves home'). Next-chord ideas show small chord diagrams instead of fret digits, and tapping one (or a history chord) opens it in the Chords lookup.
