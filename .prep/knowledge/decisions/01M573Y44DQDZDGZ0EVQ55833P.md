A string with clean partials counts as played when its partials stand out: the second-largest prominence (peak minus the median spectrum around it, outside the window's main lobe, out to a semitone) over its clean partials that no other string's estimated partial explains, ≥ 20 dB (`PRESENT_PROMINENCE_DB`). On synthetic strums (`examples/presence_eval.rs`, 300 per scenario, with and without the previous strum ringing): played strings ≥ 39 dB, muted ≤ ~10 dB at p95; muted caught 778/780, played wrongly missing 3/~3800 (guitar G3).

Tried and rejected: energy rise before/after the strum (the previous strum's ringing, and a muted string's estimate wandering ±60 cents onto other strings' peaks, made played and muted overlap); median prominence and a ±1-semitone background (low partials' main lobes are wider than a semitone; pluck-position nulls silence single partials); "unique energy" / residual reduction for B3/E4 (inverted: the free partial absorbs the known partials' errors).

Guitar B3 and E4 have no clean partials: every partial coincides with E2's (and A2's) series, so a muted B3/E4 cannot be told from those harmonics. Their presence is unknown and their reading is always shown.
